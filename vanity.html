Why Traditional Vanities Fail

Moisture is the enemy of standard bathroom vanities. Water splashes, steam, and leaks cause wood to swell, laminate to delaminate, and particleboard to disintegrate — often within just a few years.

Our Solution: True Porcelain-Clad Vessels

We wrap the entire visible surface (and critical edges) in dense, non-porous large-format porcelain — the same high-performance material we use in our KBRS shower systems.

  • ✅ Zero water absorption
  • ✅ Highly resistant to stains, scratches, and heat
  • ✅ Minimal grout lines for a clean, modern look
  • ✅ Extremely durable in real wet environments
  • ✅ Custom sizes, shapes, and configurations available
