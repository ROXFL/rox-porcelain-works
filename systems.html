Why Our Systems Are Different

We don’t install traditional mortar beds or cheap sheet membranes. Every shower is built using KBRS factory-waterproofed components — eliminating the most common points of failure.

Tile-Basin® Ready-to-Tile Pans

Pre-sloped, factory-waterproofed shower bases with integrated HardCurb®. Available with center, left, or right point drains.

ShowerSlope® Pre-Sloped Bases

Cut-to-fit on site for maximum flexibility. Perfect when exact dimensions are needed.

Linear Drain Systems

Low-profile linear drains with stylish grates in multiple finishes. Ideal for modern, barrier-free wet rooms.

Large-Format Porcelain Finish

Fewer grout lines, non-porous surface, extremely durable and easy to maintain.
